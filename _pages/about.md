I am a founding member of the technical staff at <a href='https://www.datologyai.com/'>DatologyAI</a>, where I spend my time conducting research aimed at understanding how deep models learn from data. Prior to that, I was an AI Resident at [Meta AI](https://ai.facebook.com) working with [Ari Morcos](http://www.arimorcos.com). 
I hold a masters' in Math and Computer Science from the [African Institute For Mathematical Sciences (AIMS)](https://nexteinstein.org/) (the [African Master's in Machine Intelligence (AMMI)](https://aimsammi.org/)).
